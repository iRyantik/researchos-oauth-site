ResearchOS Drive Sync

ResearchOS Drive Sync helps an authorized user monitor revisions to selected Google Drive research models and synchronize validated results with a private research workspace.

What it does

  • Detects changes to registered Google Drive financial model files.
  • Reads authorized model files to verify worksheet outputs, formulas, and relevant calculations.
  • Records validated model versions and working estimates in a private research database.
  • Connects approved research updates to a private Notion knowledge workspace.

Access and permissions

The Google Drive integration requests read-only Drive access. It does not use this permission to edit or delete Google Drive files. Only the account owner or authorized users can initiate the private research workflow. This is not a publicly available consumer service.

Privacy and control

Google Drive information is processed for model monitoring, validation, and research synchronization. Read the Privacy Policy for details on usage, retention, security, and revocation.
