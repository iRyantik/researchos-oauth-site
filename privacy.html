Privacy Policy

This policy describes how ResearchOS Drive Sync (“the application”) accesses and processes Google user data as part of a private investment research workflow.

1. Data accessed

With the user's authorization, the application may read Google Drive file metadata (including names, identifiers, revision information and timestamps) and the contents of research model files needed for validation. The requested Google Drive scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly. This scope can technically permit reading other accessible Drive files; the workflow is designed to process registered research files.

2. Purposes and use

The application uses this information to detect version changes, check spreadsheets and formula outputs, validate financial model data, maintain research file references, and synchronize validated working estimates to the user's private ResearchOS environment. Information may be transmitted to a private Supabase backend for these operations and may be referenced from a private Notion research workspace.

3. Storage and retention

File identifiers, revision records, validation results, model mappings, relevant extracted data and audit logs may be retained in the private research database for operational continuity and traceability. Files remain in Google Drive unless the user separately saves or exports a copy. OAuth refresh credentials are stored in protected backend secrets rather than public website code. The application operator can remove retained application records on request, subject to legitimate operational and legal retention needs.

4. Sharing and transfers

The application is intended for private use. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Data may be processed by the infrastructure services used to operate the workflow, including Google Drive, Supabase and Notion where applicable, only to deliver the research functions described here. Data is not shared with unrelated third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is not used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

6. Security

Access is restricted through account authorization and backend access controls. The application uses encrypted HTTPS connections for Google API and backend communications. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. User choice and deletion

You can revoke the application's Google access at any time through Google Account third-party access settings. To request deletion of ResearchOS records associated with your account or ask a question about data handling, email iryantik.xyz@gmail.com. Revoking access stops future authorized retrieval but does not by itself erase records previously synchronized to the private backend.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's access or data processing changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

9. Contact

ResearchOS Drive Sync operator: iryantik.xyz@gmail.com.
